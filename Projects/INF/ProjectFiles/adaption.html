System Adaption in the Agricultural Supply Chain

In an agricultural supply chain, farmers, processors, logistics staff and retailers all work with different information, so the system should support these different roles. Logistics staff may need collection schedules, farm locations and refrigerated truck availability, while retailers may need expected delivery dates, stock availability and product quantities. The system could also be adapted for mobile use in rural areas, allow offline data capture where internet access is unreliable, use local units such as hectares, kilograms and tonnes, and send alerts when a harvest is delayed or when processing capacity is likely to be exceeded. These customisations can reduce data-capture errors, save time, improve communication between supply-chain partners, help prevent overproduction and shortages, reduce spoilage and transport inefficiencies, and make the system easier for staff to use. The organisation is more likely to achieve better user adoption, more accurate information and better decision-making across the entire supply chain.
